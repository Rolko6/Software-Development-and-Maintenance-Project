Assistant implementation choices and reasons:

- Raw `NaN` from the device, because only then does the gateway fix get exercised by the simulator. This was the user's choice from two options.
- Faults are opt-in and the fault layer wraps the model only when a rate is above 0, so existing tests, CI and the smoke test see an unchanged device.
- Rejected readings are counted but not timed in `gateway_request_duration_seconds`: the handler has no start time. This is recorded as a limitation, and the monitoring guide was corrected where it said otherwise.
- The fault-rate sum check tolerates `1e-9`, after the assistant found that 2026 two-decimal combinations summing to exactly 1 exceed 1 in floating point.

Human decision and reviewer: Rolko6 chose the approach and the `NaN` behaviour, approved the plan, and reviewed and committed each step. No second reviewer is recorded. Documentation was written by the assistant at the user's request; review of it is not recorded at the time of writing.
